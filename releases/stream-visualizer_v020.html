Timeline

Flow landscape

How to read it. Floor: left to right is price relative to where the mid is at the playhead; front to back is how many seconds ago. Height and colour: net aggressor flow in that spot, green where buyers hit the asks, red where sellers hit the bids, flat where nothing traded. The white line on the floor is where the mid price actually was. A ridge that runs straight back at one price while the white line stays put is flow being absorbed there; a ridge that slides sideways along the white line is flow that pushed price. Drag to rotate (sideways only on a phone), use the buttons for fixed angles, and move the slider or press Play to walk through time; Live follows the market. Feed health

Latency = (receive time + clock offset) − exchange event time. The offset is measured against Binance's server clock (NTP-style, lowest round-trip of 5 samples, refreshed every minute), so these numbers are not skewed by your device clock. Late = corrected latency above 1s. A stream is flagged silent when it exceeds its normal gap.
