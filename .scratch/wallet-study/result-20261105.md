## Reading

- No feature passed block B after the Holm correction (the smallest Holm p is 0.539), so nothing reached C. The only raw p under 0.05 is blockShare in B (rho 0.12, raw p 0.049); it is 0.009 in C, with the opposite-or-nil sign pattern, and 11 features were tested: the result is what chance gives.
- With about 300 windows per block the standard error of a rank correlation is about 0.06 and the smallest effect that could pass was about 0.17 (plan, section 6). Every |rho| observed is at most 0.12: this says there is no effect larger than roughly 0.15 on this tape at this horizon, not that wallet information is useless.
- Not tested by this plan, and the likely reasons for the null: the horizon (the paper's gains are at 1 s; ours is 5 minutes, where any flow effect has had time to wash out); a hot cohort of 257 wallets chosen as the in-sample top decile of 2,573 (some of that is luck and regresses; the persistence of markouts from A to B and C was not part of the registered plan); a sample that is 99 hours of mostly volatile stretches.
- Nothing here changes the Reader, the plan or the self-test.
