# Power of the collector test (2026-11-06)

Context: grilling session on `docs/research/free-data-streams.md`. Agreed that "no confirmed signal" for the 7 collectors means unknown, not null. This note is step 1 of the agreed order.

## Data
- 184 saved sessions over 10 days (23 Oct to 6 Nov 2026), 116 recorded hours; 177 sessions / 113 hours carry collector data. Plan minimums (24 h, 2 days) met.
- 158 sessions are auto-recordings, so they lean toward volatile stretches.

## Sample size
Horizon 5 min, step 10 s: neighbouring samples are heavily correlated. Counted one independent sample per non-overlapping 5-minute block (the most generous reading): about 1,350 blocks in total, about 680 in the confirming half.

## Smallest detectable correlation (80% power)
- Confirming half, alpha 0.05: about 0.11. At the plan's `minAbsRho` 0.08 its power is about 55%.
- Deciding half, correction for 21 features (about 0.0024 per feature): about 0.15. A true 0.08 passes it about 17% of the time.
- Joint chance for a real 0.08 feature to clear both halves: about 9%.
- Detecting 0.08 at 80% power in the deciding half needs about 2,350 blocks per half, roughly 390 recorded hours (have about 113).

## Caveats
- Real effective sample is smaller (volatility clusters over hours), so these figures are optimistic.
- The evaluation itself was not run: the full `--merge` over 184 sessions ran out of memory, then timed out, then was killed for system memory.

## Consequence
The 40-hour placeholder stop rule for a new collector is too short: at 40 h the confirming half has about 240 blocks and sees only about 0.18. A stop rule must be tied to a detectable effect, not to a fixed number of hours.
