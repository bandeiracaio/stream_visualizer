# Archive: studies that are finished or no longer fed by the app

Moved here on 2026-11-10 (Trader step 2a, `.scratch/trader/spec.md`). Nothing was re-run to write this page; the results are copied from each study's own findings file.

**The scripts here are allowed to stop working.** They import app files (`src/js/*.js`) that later steps remove, and the data they read (collector records, labels) is no longer produced. Their shared helpers that the kept scripts still need were moved to `lib/` (`session-io.mjs`: `readInput`; `sigma.mjs`: `sigmaOf`, `sideDir`, `forwardMove`). Paths below are inside `archive/`; the study folders are in `archive/scratch/`.

All studies ran on the saved sessions in `sessions/` (212 files, 134.6 recorded hours, 18 Oct to 8 Nov 2026, BTC, 96% volatility-triggered auto-recordings). Every number is small-sample.

| Study | Question | Result | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collector evaluation | Does any of 21 collector features, taken now, say something about the next 5 minutes of Binance mark volatility that the previous 5 minutes do not? | Nothing: none of 21 passed the deciding half (smallest p 0.069). Power only covers effects of about 0.15 or more. | `analyze-collectors.mjs`, `scratch/new-collector-gate/` |
| Wallet study (registered 2026-11-05, run once) | Do 11 features of Hyperliquid taker wallets predict the next 5-minute move? | No feature passed the deciding block after Holm correction (smallest Holm p 0.539). An exploratory follow-up found wallets with a good 10 s markout keep about 1 bps later (rank correlation 0.08 to 0.13), not a tested edge. | `analyze-wallets.mjs`, `analyze-wallets-persistence.mjs`, `scratch/wallet-study/` |
| Liquidation reversal | After a big one-sided liquidation cluster, does Binance price come back more than after matched moves without one? | Exploration only: after 242 clusters of $100k or more, price tended to bounce about one typical move within a minute, but the matched control was inconclusive (14, 7, 2 pairs). The registered test was **closed without being run** (amendment 2026-11-10). | `analyze-liquidations.mjs`, `scratch/liquidation-study/` |
| Venue lead-lag | Which venue's price moves first, and does a venue's gap to Binance predict its next move? | At 5 s the Binance spot, Hyperliquid, OKX spot and Bybit spot lag the Binance perp; the gap predicts nothing. 1 s unreadable at the 2 s record cadence. | `analyze-venue-leadlag.mjs`, `scratch/venue-leadlag/` |
| Basis, funding, open interest | Do basis, funding spreads, OI change and long/short ratios predict the next 5 / 15 minute move or liquidation dollars? | Nothing survived both halves; funding against direction is the only lead. | `analyze-basis-funding.mjs`, `scratch/basis-funding-oi/` |
| Data inventory | What can each analysis use: sessions, hours, collectors, quality? | 212 sessions, 134.6 h, 204 auto-recordings; 197 with a complete quality verdict; 46 sessions after the liquidation cutoff. Counts only. | `inventory-sessions.mjs`, `scratch/data-inventory/` |
| Venue volume | How much BTC each venue trades and its share. | A collector spec (`tradeVol`, `vol24`) and a script that downloads free public history; no finding recorded. | `venue-volume-history.mjs`, `scratch/venue-volume/` |
| Reader research | What does the literature say about the absorption, exhaustion, burst and wall families? | Models, not detectors: no published definition or threshold for crypto perpetuals; ideas such as impact shortfall and order-flow imbalance to replace fixed bands. Fetched claims marked VERIFIED, others UNVERIFIED. | `scratch/reader-research/` |
| Self-revising Reader | Can the Reader revise its own assumptions from data, only when sure? | Built (register, baselines, drift flags, threshold proposals, sigma scale). Superseded by the Trader spec: removed from the app in step 2c. | `scratch/self-revising-reader/`, ADRs 0001 to 0004 in `docs/adr/` |
| Labelling | Can episodes be judged by eye cheaply (label queue, scoreboard, blinded panel)? | Built as tools (label queue v093, scoreboard v119, `panel-label.mjs`, `reader-scoreboard.mjs`, `reader-robustness.mjs`, `analyze-episodes.mjs`); no verdict on the Reader came out of them. Weekly review notes are in `scratch/weekly-review/`. | `scratch/label-queue/`, `scratch/scoreboard-view/`, `scratch/weekly-review/` |

Registered tests closed without being run (2026-11-10): the liquidation test (plan in `scratch/liquidation-study/plan.md`) and the three trade-system tests `prereg-xv-fade-hl.md`, `prereg-book-depth.md`, `prereg-book-depth-split.md` (still in `.scratch/trade-system/`). No test-set outcome was looked at; the Trader's live record replaces them.

The batch trade-system harness (2026-11-11, Trader step 3): `design-harness-batch.mjs` is the old `design-harness.mjs`, unchanged. It computed a design's trades from whole-session arrays (session sigma, session median flow, end-of-session episode groups, dropping trades the history could not finish). `design-harness.mjs` now runs the same designs through `src/js/system-core.js` instead. **This copy may stop running**: it imports `./compare-detections.mjs`, `./lib/` and `./sample-designs.mjs` by relative path (run it from a copy in the repo root), reads the old fact caches under `.scratch/trade-system/cache*`, and depends on the Reader files as they are today. `.scratch/trader/step3/baseline-trades.jsonl` holds what it produced for the 14 frozen designs before the change.
