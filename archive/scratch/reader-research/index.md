## Summary
The literature gives the Reader models, not detectors. None of the four families has a published definition, threshold or reliability study for crypto perpetuals; the Reader's cut-offs are internal and the sources neither support nor refute them. What the sources do give is a way to replace fixed bands with expectations: price change is linear in order flow imbalance with a slope set by depth (Cont, Kukanov, Stoikov, arXiv:1011.6402), impact is partly transient with a latent book behind the visible one (Donier et al., arXiv:1412.0141), trade clustering is self-exciting (Hawkes; Bacry et al., arXiv:1502.04592), and flow intensity measures such as VPIN are contested (Andersen and Bondarenko). Exchange documentation shows two data limits that matter now: Binance's liquidation stream sends only the latest liquidation per symbol per 1000 ms, so liquidation counts are lower bounds; Hyperliquid pushes book snapshots at most every 0.5 s per block, gives the number of orders per level, and names buyer and seller wallets on every trade. Most improvements are testable on saved sessions with the replay tools and no labels.

## Ranked hypotheses
1. Family: absorption. Hypothesis: replacing the fixed "stalled" band with an impact shortfall (observed move minus lambda times signed flow, in sigma) separates real absorption from low-flow quiet windows. Expected effect: fewer detections in thin flow, more in heavy flow with small moves; passive-side refill higher in detections than controls. Tag: DOABLE TODAY. Test: fit lambda per venue, compute shortfall for every window, calibrate k by shuffling signed volumes across the session, then compare forward 10/30/60 s moves and refill against matched controls (block bootstrap by session); byte-compare with current detections via compare-detections.mjs.
2. Family: exhaustion. Hypothesis: flow decay to half of the previous window carries little forward information because signed flow is persistent; a stratified test will show a small contrast. Expected effect: forward move after low-ratio windows differs little from high-ratio windows; if so, demote the family or require stronger items. Tag: DOABLE TODAY. Test: stratify every 1 s tick by z_was and ratio; report mean signed forward move per stratum; sweep `exhDecay` .3 to .7; circular-shift null; optional Hawkes residual intensity version.
3. Family: burst. Hypothesis: a time-of-day-adjusted baseline and a Hawkes endogeneity measure improve the gate (fewer scheduled-time false alarms; endogenous vs exogenous bursts differ in outcome). Tag: DOABLE TODAY (needs several sessions for seasonality). Test: compare excursions beyond a Poisson bound (seasonal baseline) with episodes; split outcomes by branching ratio.
4. Family: wall. Hypothesis: the share of walls pulled before price touches them (size drops with no matching trades) is high and depends on distance and age; walls that survive approach show reflection, pulled ones do not. Expected effect: a wall-quality score (held, pulled, consumed) separates informative from decorative walls. Tag: DOABLE TODAY (250 ms levels plus trades; order count `n` on Hyperliquid if stored). Test: approach analysis by distance bucket vs shuffled-size null; mid movement after held vs pulled.
5. Family: liquidation cascade (new). Hypothesis: forced-flow count (Binance snapshots plus followed-wallet liquidation fills) with same-side price move and an open-interest drop identifies cascades without labels; Hyperliquid partial liquidations recur about every 30 s per large position. Tag: DOABLE TODAY at coarse resolution; notional-weighted NEEDS NEW DATA. Test: windows meeting the definition vs controls; check OI drop and the 30 s periodicity in followed wallets.
6. Family: iceberg / refill (new, subtype of absorption). Hypothesis: repeated same-level refills after fills, especially by one Hyperliquid wallet on the passive side, mark hidden or sliced liquidity. Tag: partly DOABLE TODAY (proxy), strict identification NEEDS NEW DATA. Test: cumulative taker volume at a price exceeding displayed size plus adds, against shuffled-depth null.
7. Family: cross-cutting. Hypothesis: other venues' price response to the same flow (Coinbase, Bybit, OKX, spot) adds evidence for absorption and burst beyond the existing `cross` item. Tag: DOABLE TODAY. Test: lead-lag with circular-shift significance on sessions with `ext`.
8. Family: sweeps. Hypothesis: sweep size as a share of local depth is a better evidence item than a raw count. Tag: DOABLE TODAY. Test: reconcile sweep summaries with levels removed from consecutive depth snapshots.
9. Family: spoof-like pattern. Hypothesis: nothing beyond a pulled-wall subtype is supportable; intent cannot be detected. Tag: genuine layering NEEDS NEW DATA. No test; report only pull rates.
