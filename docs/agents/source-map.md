# Source map

Reference, read when you touch that area. Specs are in `.scratch/<feature>/`, decisions in `docs/adr/`, terms in `CONTEXT.md`. The terms **pure** and **display only** are defined in `CLAUDE.md`.

Pure files (checked: no DOM, storage or socket use): every `*-core.js`, `hooks.js`, `views.js`, `session-quality.js`, and the Reader engine files `reader-episodes`, `reader-engine`, `reader-selftest`, and `broker.js` (it fetches and signs only through injected functions). Every other file touches the page.

## Seams

- `src/js/hooks.js` (`HOOKS.on(name, fn)` / `HOOKS.emit(name, ...args)`): the way a late-loaded module extends an owner's function. Names: `renderReader` (end of `renderReader`), `renderAccount` (end of `renderAccount`), `logout` (end of `logout`), `bnDepth` (start of `bnDepth`, before the book applies it; no subscriber since the book collector moved to the worker). Subscribers run in script-load order; an error in one is logged and contained.
- `src/js/views.js` (`VIEWS`): the registry of views. `VIEWS.area(id, label, icon)` declares a navigation area with its icon path (`VIEWS.icons()` feeds `GICON`); `VIEWS.register({id, label, area, slot?, minIntervalMs?, render?, onOpen?})` adds a view (registration order = display order). Views with the same `slot` share one render and one throttle; the frame loop calls `VIEWS.tick`. `VIEWS.onOpen(id, fn)` adds an open action from a later module; `setTab` calls `VIEWS.open`. Outside the registry: the dirty-flag block (market / tape / raw) and `renderSlow` (1 s).

## Reader

- `src/js/reader-engine.js`: order-flow engine and the legacy one-off event detectors; drives the episode layer once a second. Replay is `READER_ENGINE.replayEngine(inputs, cfg)`. Recording of the page's inputs is not here any more: `chunk-core.js` records what it feeds the engine (`startRecording` / `stopRecording` / `trimRecording` remain in the engine, unused by the page since v142).
- `src/js/reader-episodes.js`: episode state machines and evidence checklists for absorption / exhaustion / burst / wall. Tunables in `READER_EPISODES.DEFAULTS` (also `READER.cfg.ep` at runtime). Fast and mid readings of one event are separate episodes on purpose; the groups layer (`groupGapMs`) presents them as one.
- `src/js/reader-tools.js` (loaded late, called from `renderReader`): record / save / load / replay (current or edited thresholds) and the timeline, a read-only picture of episodes over price. Sigma (`sigmaBps`) is in `docs/adr/0004`.
- `src/js/reader-selftest.js`: simulator scenarios; the build runs them and fails if any fail. A new engine behaviour gets a scenario here.
- `src/js/system-core.js` (global `SYSTEM`, pure; Trader step 3): a trade system is one JSON object (context, region, trigger, thesis with invalidation, exit, risk). `SYSTEM.facts({engine, lateMs})` takes the engine's inputs (`feed(record)`, or `trade` / `quote` / `book` / `sweep` / `advance`) and returns one snapshot a second: price, the engine's live sigma (the read-only `engine.sigmaPeek`; `sigmaBps` refreshes a cache keyed to engine time, so calling it from outside moves detections), taker flow, book imbalance, episode groups as first seen, market state. `SYSTEM.create(def).step(snapshot)` returns intents (`enter`, `exit`, `invalidate`, `skip`); the caller reports fills (`filled`, `closed`, `cancelled`, `end`). Also `validate`, `id`, `size`. The page includes it and self-tests it (`system:*` scenarios); nothing calls it live until the runner (step 4). `design-harness.mjs` runs the same file over recorded sessions (snapshots cached in `.scratch/trade-system/cache-snap/`, replayed with `lateMs` 5 minutes because recorded timestamps are up to 5 s out of order between kinds of input and a backlog can be minutes late; the page keeps the 1 s default). `--legacy-facts` feeds it the old batch harness's look-ahead facts; `.scratch/trader/step3/compare.mjs` uses that to check it against the batch harness's trades (`baseline-trades.jsonl`).
- `src/js/runner-core.js` (`RUNNER`, pure; Trader step 4), `src/js/systems-default.js` (`SYSTEMS_DEFAULT`, seven fixed paper systems) and `src/js/runner.js` (page). `RUNNER.create({systems, twins, equity, feeBps, riskCap, seed, journal}).step(snapshot)` gives each system and each of its 20 random-entry twins its own ledger, fills at the far side of the Binance quote in the snapshot with a fee a side, caps total open risk across systems (twins not capped), and returns journal rows; `RUNNER.evaluate(rows)` gives the figures and the percentile among twins. No clock, timers or random numbers: the same snapshots give the same journal. A position open at a restart is written as `interrupted` and left out of the figures. The snapshot carries the Binance quote (`q`, `FACTS_VERSION` 2); an instance made with `opt.forced` takes `force({t, side})` signals at step time. In the page, `feeds.js` routes every engine input through one `SYSTEM.facts` accumulator (`TFACTS`; `READER` inherits the engine, so only the input methods differ) and hands the snapshots to `trSink`; `runner.js` keeps the journal in IndexedDB `sv-trader`, starts a new runner after a data gap over 30 s, and exposes the journal, export and reset to the Trader home. BTC only, switch saved as `trOn`. It sends no order: brokers, `placeOrder` and `SIM` are untouched.
- `src/js/promotion-core.js` (`PROMO`, pure; Trader step 7): the promotion rule of spec 4.7 as built (paper to demo: 300 trades, 10 UTC days, mean net above 0, mean net R above at least 19 of 20 twins that each have 100 trades, 60% positive days; demo to live: 100 demo trades, 5 days, demo fill worse than paper by at most half the paper edge), counted on trades made live by the page since the last move into the stage. `eligibility(rows, stageLog, id)` gives the criteria (now / needed / met, text) and `eligible`; `lossLimit(rows, {id, riskFrac, equity0}, cap, day)` is the automatic move down of a live system; `apply(stageLog, move, rows)` validates a move and returns the new log or the reason. The stage log (entries `{sys, from, to, t, by, reason?, cap?, figures}`) is the `stages` store of IndexedDB `sv-trader` (version 2), goes into the journal export and is cleared only by Reset. `RUNNER.create` takes `stages` ({id: stage}); `RUNNER.driver` takes `move({sys, to})`, applies it just before the next snapshot, and `drain()` returns `[{sys, to, at}]`, which `runner.js` writes into the chunk (`moves`; `runner.stages` holds the stages at the start of the run) so `--replay-run` hands them back at the same snapshot. Enter and trade rows carry `stage` (when entered) and `fill` (always `paper` until step 8; a demo row will also carry `paperNetBps`); rows before step 7 read as paper / paper, also in `CHUNK.firstDiff`. `trMove` / `trAppendStage` / `trLossCheck` are in `runner.js`; the Promotion block and its confirmation panel are in `trader-view.js` (promotion buttons are off on a touch-only device). No order is sent: demo and live still fill on paper until steps 8 and 9.
- `src/js/exec-core.js` (`EXEC`, pure; Trader step 8): demo execution as a state machine per asset (idle, entering, stopping, holding, exiting, settling, stuck; overlays: blocked, fault, killed). Inputs: the runner's rows (`onRow`), the exchange's answers (`reply`, `tick`), its state (`start`, `poll`); outputs (`drain()`): requests, `mirror` rows, faults. It never fetches and refuses to produce a request unless the base URL is the demo host (`ALLOW_LIVE` is false until step 9). Rules: market entry, reduce-only stop from the real fill (rejected or unconfirmed in 3 s: close at once), unknown result looked up by client id (still unknown after 5 s: fault), one exchange position at a time (`account busy`), blocks (quote age, clock offset, one-way mode, minimum size, kill switch, fault, unreadable exchange), size capped at 5,000 notional, a leftover own position closed at start, a foreign one blocks. It carries its own fake exchange and the `exec:*` scenarios. `EXEC.merge(rows)` is the view the promotion rule reads: a paper trade that also filled on the exchange both ways reads as a demo trade (the stored paper rows are unchanged, so `--replay-run` still matches). `src/js/exec.js` (page): sends the requests through `BROKER.binance` (`marketCid`, `stopAlgo` on `/fapi/v1/algoOrder`, `cancelAlgoCid`, `orderByCid`, `algoByCid`, `tradesOf`, `oneWay`, `netPosition`, `openAll`), polls every 3 s while holding, keeps the kill switch (`sv1:exKill`), the connection check (`sv1:exCheck`, valid 24 h, also needed before a user promotion to demo) and `sv1:exOpen` (a position of ours was open: ownership after a reload). Mirror rows and the fault log are the `mirror` and `faults` stores of IndexedDB `sv-trader` (version 3; Reset clears the mirror rows, never the faults) and the chunk's `exec` list (recorded facts, not replayed). Exchange notes: `.scratch/trader/step8/exchange-notes.md`.

## App shell

- `src/js/app.js`: charts, navigation, account state (`ACC`), the sim wrappers, `placeOrder` and the page timers. The rest of what used to live there is in deep modules behind small interfaces (v127, display only; see below).
  - `src/js/paper-broker-core.js` (`PAPER`, pure): the paper broker. State, symbol, clock and quotes (`ctx = {sym, now, q:{bid, ask, mark}}`) are passed in; functions mutate the `SIM`-shaped state in place (same shape `account-info.js` / `order-history.js` read) and return data, never text. Fees 0.0002 maker / 0.0005 taker. `app.js` keeps thin `sim*` wrappers and `simCtx()`, which supplies `accSym()`, `Date.now()` and `M.bnBid/bnAsk/bnMark`. Self-tests prefixed `paper broker:`.
  - `src/js/broker.js` (`BROKER`): the broker seam. `BROKER.sim(deps)` and `BROKER.binance(deps)` both give `place(order)`, `cancel(id)`, `cancelAll()` (this asset only) and `snapshot()`; signing / fetch and the sim wrappers are injected. Validation (filter rounding, minimums, the live cap) and the live `confirm()` stay above the seam in `placeOrder`: validate, confirm, `broker().place`. `ACC`, the 3 s live refresh and `refreshSoon` are unchanged. Self-tests `broker:` use a stubbed `signed`; no real Binance request has been made through it yet.
  - `src/js/micro-core.js` (`MICRO`, pure): microstructure data (`topN`, `imbalance`, `heatSnap`, `microprice`, sweep grouping: same ms and side is one sweep). `MICRO.create({onSweep})` holds the state; `app.js` owns one instance `MI` (`MI.hist`, `MI.heatBn`, `MI.heatHl`, `MI.sweeps`) and the 250 ms timers, and the `draw*` / `render*` functions read it. Self-tests `micro:`.
  - `src/js/market-core.js` (`MARKET`, pure): the history invariants (5-minute `KEEP` window, 250 ms mid row, 500 ms cvd row, trade bookkeeping, reset values). `app.js` still owns the live objects `M`, `trades`, `cvd`, `cvdHist`, `midHist`, `health`, so every reader is unchanged; writers go through `MARKET`. Not done on purpose: `feeds.js` still assigns `M.x` directly and increments `health.*`. Who writes what: `.scratch/market-store/map.md`. Self-tests `market:`.
  - `src/js/trading-view.js` (page): the Trading layout (module catalog, `addModule`, order ticket, `renderTrading`; `tradingInit()` is called from `app.js` at startup). `src/js/reader-cards.js` (page): Reader episode cards (`epCard`, `epWho`, `epEvidence`, `renderEpisodes` (hides a wall card younger than `epFilter.minWallMs`, display only; the header select `epMinAge`, saved under `epMinAge`), `rdFilter`, `epFilter`, `rdView` / `RD`). Both load before `app.js`; `renderReader` stays in `app.js`.
  - `src/js/trader-core.js` (`TRADER`, pure; Trader step 5a, display only) and `src/js/trader-view.js` (page): the first view (`overview`, shown as Trader). `TRADER.cells` gives the seven parts of a system (context, region, trigger reading against its threshold, thesis, exit, risk, evaluation with the twins' ticks) from the instance's `state()`, the runner's `state()` entry and `RUNNER.evaluate`; `markers` (entry, exit by reason, invalidation, skip, open and interrupted trades), `boxSeries` (the range box from the price history), `dayBars`, `skipSummary`, `journal` (latest 200), `status`, `describe` (a definition in plain words). `trader-view.js` draws the status line, a card per system (seven columns from 1400 px), the detail of the selected system (`trSel`), the price chart from `READER.priceHistory("bn")` (window `trWin`, redrawn only when its data changed; the view model is rebuilt at most twice a second, only while the view is open) and the journal. Self-tests `trader:`. `RUNNER.state()` entries carry `inst` (the instance's own state) and `RUNNER.risk()` gives open risk against the cap.
  - `src/js/overview.js`: the market, event clock, Reader, recording and account cards, below the Trader sections of the first view.
- `src/js/feeds.js` (loaded before `app.js`; moved verbatim out of it): the market-reader instance and hooks, the Binance and Hyperliquid socket handlers and book maintenance. It reads and writes `app.js` globals (`M`, `S`, `addTrade`, the DOM) by name, and `collectors.js` reaches `bnBook`, `hlBookT`, `HL.ev`. This is a locality move, not a narrow interface, because there is no second source to put behind one.
- Navigation (Trader step 5b; the earlier spec is `.scratch/app-reorganization/spec.md`): five areas (`GROUPS`, built from `VIEWS.area` / `VIEWS.register` in `app.js`, in this order): Trader (overview, shown as "Trader"), Market (micro, market, reader = Episodes), Recordings (record, replay, armmode), Account (trading, history), Diagnostics (health, streams, raw, engine, signals). View ids and `data-tab` values did not change; only areas, labels and order. A view is what `data-tab` on a `<section>` names; every view is one `VIEWS.register` in `app.js`, and the views with `slot:"reader"` (reader, signals, replay, record, engine) are drawn by `renderReader`. A saved last view (`view`, `lastView`) that is no longer a view opens the Trader. Phone: fixed bottom bar with the five areas, header two rows (about 95 px) when the area has a sub-row; PC: left sidebar. The header status chips (`#liveChips`) live in Feed health. Messages from record / save / load / replay go to every `.rtmsg` element.
- Order history and account info (spec `.scratch/order-history/spec.md`): `src/js/history-core.js` (`HIST`): one row shape for orders / fills / income from Binance and the simulated broker, the account in the same shape, totals, 7-day pieces, merge, CSV columns. The paper broker keeps `SIM.log` (orders, fills, income; capped) beside its own arrays, which stay as they are. `src/js/account-info.js` (header Account panel, left half of the button) and `src/js/order-history.js` (Account > History: Orders / Fills / Income, symbol, period, totals, CSV) are read-only (GET only), load when opened or on Refresh and never on a timer, clear their caches on the `logout` hook and load on `VIEWS.onOpen`. Display only. The Binance paths were tested with a stubbed `fetch`.

### Header

- Account split button (`#accSplit`, `src/js/account-button.js`): a left half (the account info panel, or the connect panel when nothing is connected; shows mode and unrealised PnL) and a caret (switch / connect panel). The panels are `section.accpanel` elements in `header` holding the old content with the same element ids; `HOOKS` name `renderAccount`. On a phone the button is one coloured letter (S / D / L, + when not connected) so the header stays two rows. Its panel `#stPanel` holds the venue texts (`#bnState`, `#hlState`) and all live chips (`#stChips`, written by `sbRender` beside `#liveChips`).
- The header has no Record or Arm button since v142 (continuous recording replaced both); the recording state shows in the status chips, on the Trader home (`rrShort`) and in Recordings.
- `src/js/keyboard-lock.js`: the 🐱 `#catLock` button beside the status button swallows every key until it is clicked again.
- `src/js/status-core.js`: header status chips and the version comparison. A save-folder chip ("save folder needs OK" / "last save failed" / "no save folder") shows only while the page records; `sbSnapshot` reads `FS` from `folder-save.js` and re-queries the folder permission every 30 s while it records. Chips: `REC run, chunk N`, `chunk save failed`, `screen may sleep`. `src/js/status-bar.js` is its view (chips next to Connect / Pause, the "newer version" bar). The build stamps `APP_VERSION` (`vNNN` for a release, `dev` otherwise); the page fetches `version.json` (written by the Pages workflow) to learn the newest release.

## Recording

- Continuous recording (Trader step 6a): `src/js/chunk-core.js` (`CHUNK`, pure: the input pipeline that thins quotes to one per 100 ms per venue, records and cuts chunks by input time (30 min) or count (600,000), the run replay, `check`, `firstDiff`, file names) and `src/js/run-record.js` (page side: the `RR` state, run begin / end on `READER.reset` and disconnect, saving each chunk gzip into the save folder and the browser's storage for 24 h, the switch `recOn`, reconnect after a reload, `rr.last` and the cut-short mark, Web Lock `sv-connected` + screen wake lock). `feeds.js` builds `PIPE` and routes `READER.onTrade/onQuote/...` through it into `TFACTS`; `RUNNER.driver` (`runner-core.js`) owns the runner's gap rule and carry-over, `runner.js` feeds it from `trSink`. A chunk file = a session file + `run, seq, final, why, runner{on,cfg,ids,carry}, engineCfg, lateMs, rows, changed, prevMissing`. `node design-harness.mjs --replay-run <files|folder>` replays each run and compares the journal rows (exit 1 on a difference). The page supplies no market state to the accumulator, so none is stored. Settings that move inside a chunk (`changed`) end what a replay can follow.
- Auto-record, arming, Arm mode, the re-arm prompt and the overnight checklist were removed in v142 (step 6b): `auto-record*.js`, `arm-mode.js`, `arm-memory*.js`, `overnight-*.js`, the header Arm and Record buttons, the `pre` block (old files with `pre` still load; the field is ignored). The page holds a Web Lock `sv-connected` and the screen wake lock while connected (`run-record.js`). ADR 0005 is superseded.
- `src/js/recordings.js`: Saved recordings, an IndexedDB copy of every saved chunk or session (Share / Download / Open / Delete; chunks are listed grouped by run and deleted after 24 hours, `rrPrune`), and the "Hours recorded" grid (one cell per hour, `rcCoverage`).
- Session quality: `src/js/session-quality.js` rates how complete a recording is (minutes with data, longest silence, clock rate); saved into every session file as `quality` (plus the page's `health` notes) and shown in Saved recordings. `quality.collectors` says, per collector that was switched on (`colExpected` in `collectors.js`), how many records it wrote during the recording itself (from the trigger, so not the lead-in), the minutes with one and the longest silence (`SQ.collectors`; state none / holes / ok; holes = silence over three times its usual interval, at least 30 s; a collector writing less often than a third of the window, e.g. calendar, is not judged). `SQ.clean` (inputs and collectors) drives the warning colour; `quality.verdict` is unchanged. Files from before v111 have no `collectors`.
- Health log (`src/js/health-log.js`, loaded after `status-bar.js`; under Saved recordings): a read-only black box in `localStorage` (400 events) for tab hidden or frozen, timer gaps, computer sleep, page reopened, venue silent, price-stream drops, wake lock lost, learning restarted, and the workers' collector errors and "socket closed" notes (see Collectors).

## Collectors

Spec `.scratch/collectors/`. The object is `COLLECT` (not `COL`, which is the chart colours in `app.js`).

- `src/js/collectors-core.js` (`COLLECT`): `COLLECT.REGISTRY` has one entry per collector (`id`, `owner` page / worker, `expectMs`, `records`, `features`, `info`); `OWNER`, `EXPECT_MS`, `FEATURES`, `FEATURE_INFO` are derived from it. A new collector is one `REGISTRY` entry plus one `CONTRIB` entry, and its features also go in `FEATURE_ORDER` (a self-test fails otherwise; the order is fixed because analysis plans depend on it). `COLLECT.FEATURES` is the one list of features, shared with the analysis scripts.
- `src/js/collectors.js` (view; starts the worker): records are `["c", name, t, payload]`, written to a rolling 24 h tape (IndexedDB `sv-tape`). Every saved session carries the matching slice as `ext`, beside `inputs`; the engine never sees them. Telemetry samples the page's feeds every 10 s while connected.
- `src/js/collectors-worker.js` (always-on Web Worker, embedded as `#colWorkerSrc`): derivatives context and calendar tags by rule, plus the collectors below.
  - Book depth summaries (edges 1 / 5 / 10 / 20 bps): since v107 the worker keeps its own Binance book (REST snapshot + diff stream, `COLLECT.perpBook("bn")`) and Hyperliquid book (socket + bucketed `l2Book` every 2 s, `COLLECT.hlBookSummary`) while a recording is on, one `bookBn` / `bookHl` record a second, so a hidden tab no longer starves it; the page only sends `recording` in the worker's start / rec messages.
  - Venue prices (collector `venues`): Coinbase and Bybit perp quotes and Binance spot, opened while the page is connected (the continuous recording runs then). They feed only the market state's venue-gap reading.
  - Volatility index (collector `options`, BTC / ETH only): Deribit's DVOL every 10 s, for the market state's volatility reading.
  - Removed in v136, with their sockets, polls, settings and features: long/short and taker ratios, followed wallets, option chains, trade volume, 24 h volume, Bybit / OKX perp books and perp flow, other venues and majors' marks. Recordings and tapes written before still load; their records are ignored.
- Socket close log (`COLLECT.sockWatch`; research `.scratch/socket-reconnects/research.md`): used by `collectors-worker.js`. `watch(name, socket)` writes a "socket closed" note to the Health log on every close that was not collection ending: close code, `clean`, reason, `upMs`, `silentMs`, `msgs`, and `ours` (why our own code closed it through `shut(socket, why)`: `silent` = a silence check, `page` = the page asked; empty = the server or network). A source writes at most 5 notes an hour and the next one carries `left_out`. The page forwards the worker's note to `hlNote`.
- Episode cards show collector context: in an opened card, above "your call", a line of chips with what the collectors read when it was detected (`COLLECT.context`: each reading's deviation from its own previous 30 minutes, unusual at 1.5 standard deviations and a move worth seeing; `epCtxBar`, `colContextFor`, `colLabelCtx` at the end of `collectors.js`). Live it reads the last 75 minutes kept in memory (`CL.recent`); in a replay, the session's own `ext` (`rtReadSessionFile` keeps it; a recording made on the page gets it from the tape). Agree / disagree stores the same readings in the label as `ctx` (`{t, v:{feature:[value, deviation]}, liq:[binance, hyperliquid]}`), because the tape is pruned after 24 hours. Display only: the engine, the episode layer and detections are untouched.
